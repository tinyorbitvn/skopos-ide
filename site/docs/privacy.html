Docs

Privacy

SkoposIDE has no telemetry, no analytics and no account. The project receives no data from the app.

What stays on your computer

The kubeconfig files SkoposIDE reads, its catalog, preferences and logs stay in its folders on your computer. Passwords and tokens it keeps go to the operating system's credential store: Windows Credential Manager, the macOS Keychain or the Secret Service on Linux.

Where it connects

  • The API servers of the clusters you connect, and the metrics sources you set up for them.
  • GitHub, to check for and download updates, in builds downloaded from GitHub. The Microsoft Store build does not check: the Store updates it.
  • get.helm.sh and dl.k8s.io, when you ask SkoposIDE to download helm or kubectl.
  • The proxy you set in Preferences, if any.
  • The AI agent CLI you installed and start from SkoposIDE runs on your computer as you and talks to its model provider directly under that provider's terms. SkoposIDE no longer limits its network.

Microsoft Store

Installing SkoposIDE from the Microsoft Store is covered by Microsoft's privacy statement.
